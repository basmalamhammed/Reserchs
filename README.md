## 🧪 Research Workflow

For each research project, I aim to follow a structured workflow:

1. **Problem Definition**

   * What problem am I investigating?
   * Why is it important?

2. **Background & Literature**

   * What has already been proposed?
   * What assumptions or claims are being made?

3. **Hypothesis / Research Question**

   * What exactly am I trying to investigate?

4. **Methodology**

   * Dataset
   * Models
   * Experimental setup
   * Evaluation metrics
   * Baselines
   * Variables and controlled conditions

5. **Experiments**

   * Run controlled experiments.
   * Record configurations and results.
   * Keep experiments reproducible whenever possible.

6. **Analysis**

   * Interpret the results.
   * Compare observations with previous findings.
   * Investigate unexpected behavior.

7. **Limitations**

   * Identify sources of uncertainty.
   * Document what the experiments cannot conclude.

8. **Conclusion**

   * Summarize what the evidence supports.
   * Distinguish observations from assumptions and interpretations.

## 📊 Reproducibility

Whenever possible, experiments will include:

* Environment and dependency information
* Dataset information
* Model configuration
* Experimental parameters
* Random seeds
* Evaluation metrics
* Raw or processed results
* Code used to obtain the results

The goal is not only to obtain a result, but to make the reasoning and experimental process understandable and reproducible.
